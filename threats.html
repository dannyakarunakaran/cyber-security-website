🎣 Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers use fake emails, messages or websites to trick users into revealing personal information.

How to Stay Safe

  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Check the sender's address.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.

🦠 Malware

Click to learn more

🦠 Malware

Malware is harmful software designed to damage systems, disrupt operations or steal information.

How to Stay Safe

  • Download software from trusted sources.
  • Keep your system updated.
  • Use security software.

🔒 Ransomware

Click to learn more

🔒 Ransomware

Ransomware is malware that prevents users from accessing files and may demand payment to restore access.

How to Stay Safe

  • Keep important files backed up.
  • Avoid suspicious attachments.
  • Keep software updated.

🔑 Password Attack

Click to learn more

🔑 Password Attack

Attackers try to steal or guess passwords to gain access to user accounts.

How to Stay Safe

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Never share your password.

🌐 DDoS Attack

Click to learn more

🌐 DDoS Attack

A DDoS attack overwhelms a website or online service with traffic, making it difficult for legitimate users to access.

How to Stay Safe

  • Use network protection.
  • Monitor unusual traffic.
  • Use DDoS protection services.

👤 Identity Theft

Click to learn more

👤 Identity Theft

Identity theft occurs when someone uses another person's personal information without permission.

How to Stay Safe

  • Keep personal information private.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Be careful when sharing information online.
