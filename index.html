Stay Safe Online

Learn how to protect yourself from cyber attacks, secure your personal information, and stay safe while using the internet.

Learn About Threats

What is Cyber Security?

Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks, applications, and personal information from unauthorized access and cyber attacks.

Every day, people use the internet for communication, education, shopping, banking, entertainment, and social media. Because of this, protecting our digital information has become very important.

Why Cyber Safety Matters?

Cyber criminals may try to steal passwords, personal information, financial details, or access to online accounts. Good cyber security habits can reduce these risks.

🔐 Protect Personal Data

Keep your passwords, personal information, and private files protected from unauthorized access.

🛡️ Protect Accounts

Use strong passwords and two-factor authentication to add extra protection to your online accounts.

🌐 Safe Internet Use

Learn to identify suspicious websites, messages, emails, and links before interacting with them.

Common Cyber Threats

Understanding common cyber threats is the first step toward protecting yourself online.

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that can damage systems, steal information, or perform unwanted actions.

🔒 Ransomware

A type of malware that can prevent access to files or systems and demand payment.

🔑 Password Attacks

Attempts to guess, steal, or obtain passwords to gain unauthorized access to accounts.

Essential Cyber Safety Tips

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords using a combination of letters, numbers, and special characters. Avoid using easily guessed information.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Turn on 2FA whenever available. It provides an additional layer of security for your accounts.

3. Avoid Unknown Links

Do not click suspicious links in emails, messages, or websites. Check the source first.

4. Keep Software Updated

Install security updates for your operating system, applications, and browsers.

5. Use Security Software

Use trusted antivirus and security tools to help detect and prevent malicious software.

6. Backup Important Files

Keep backups of important documents and files so they can be recovered if something goes wrong.

🛡️ Quick Safety Checklist

Before going online, remember:

  • ✔ Use a strong and unique password
  • ✔ Enable two-factor authentication
  • ✔ Check links before clicking
  • ✔ Keep your device updated
  • ✔ Avoid sharing sensitive information
  • ✔ Use secure and trusted websites
