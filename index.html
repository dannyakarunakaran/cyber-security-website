Stay Safe Online

Learn how to protect yourself from cyber attacks and stay secure on the internet.

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails that steal information.

🦠 Malware

Harmful software that damages computers.

🔒 Ransomware

Locks your files and demands money.

Cyber Safety Tips

  • Use strong passwords (mix letters, numbers, symbols)
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  • Do not click unknown links
  • Keep software updated
  • Use antivirus protection
  • Backup your important files
