About Cyber Security

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and data from cyber attacks.

🔐 Data Protection

Cyber security protects personal and sensitive information from unauthorized access.

🌐 Online Safety

It helps people stay safe while using the internet, websites and online services.

🛡️ Threat Prevention

Security methods help prevent malware, phishing, ransomware and other cyber threats.

Why Cyber Security is Important?

  • Protects personal information
  • Prevents financial loss
  • Keeps online accounts secure
  • Protects organizations and systems
